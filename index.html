🏫 Campus

Established in 2019 by the Government of Bihar, Government Engineering College (GEC), Buxar is a premier public technical institution approved by AICTE and affiliated with Bihar Engineering University (BEU). While the college initially operated out of a mentor campus in Bakhtiyarpur, it successfully shifted to its permanent, independent campus. Spanning a well-structured area of approximately 8 to 10 acres, the new campus is located at Mahdah on Itarhi Road, safely adjacent to the Buxar Police Line. The modern campus infrastructure features a dedicated academic building equipped with smart classrooms and core engineering laboratories, a central library, an auditorium, and a workshop building. Designed to be a fully residential learning environment, the campus also hosts faculty quarters, two distinct four-story boys' hostels, and a separate girls' hostel, ensuring a self-contained and secure space for its expanding student body.
